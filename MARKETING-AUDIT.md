## Executive Summary

Full Pro Restoration has real assets — a verified BBB A+ rating, IICRC-certified technicians, active general contractor and plumbing licenses, a genuine 90-minute emergency response guarantee, bilingual (English/Spanish) capability, and 8 years of operational history. **None of these are visible to visitors.** The site is structurally functional but critically under-optimized for its core purpose: converting panicked homeowners in a damage emergency into booked service calls.

Three systemic failures define the audit:

1. **Trust collapse at the decision moment.** Operating hours in structured data read 9 AM–5 PM while the headline promises 24/7 response. This single contradiction destroys the most credible claim the business makes. No certifications, no star ratings, no testimonials, no license numbers appear on any customer-facing page.

2. **CTA architecture built for browsing, not conversion.** The homepage presents "Explore" and "Enter" as primary actions — exploration verbs targeting researchers, not homeowners with water pouring through their ceiling. No phone number is persistently pinned. No inline forms exist on service pages.

3. **Invisible competitive advantages.** The company holds a unique position: bilingual, licensed general contractor + licensed plumber + IICRC certified, operating 24/7 in a market dominated by national franchises with weak local ties. Not one of these differentiators is claimed or surfaced in the site's messaging.

Implementing the prioritized recommendations below could double conversion rate within 60 days and establish a durable local SEO advantage within 90–180 days without a redesign.
